# DRIPCYCLEX — Redefined Dark Streetwear

A luxury dark menswear and streetwear e-commerce platform built for **DRIPCYCLEX**, an Indian fashion house known for its architectural fits, heavyweight milled fabrics, and draconic monochrome visual identity.

## Brand Identity & Aesthetic

- **Theme**: Pure Black & White minimalism where **Black is the primary color** (`#000000` / `#050505`).
- **Logo**: The signature DRIPCYCLEX draconic crest with sharp distressed chrome "DC" typography and a vivid crimson "X" accent.
- **Accents**: High-contrast white typography, dark obsidian cards, sharp zinc hairline borders, and subtle crimson highlights.

## Curated 3-Piece Showcase (The Trinity Drop)

Rather than overwhelming users with excessive fast-fashion SKUs, DRIPCYCLEX focuses on a curated 3-piece foundation:
1. **DRIPCYCLEX Heavyweight Cyber Tee** — 280 GSM combed cotton with drop-shoulder cut and draconic screen-printed back.
2. **DRIPCYCLEX Signature Stealth Hoodie** — 420 GSM brushed fleece with double-layered structural hood and chrome crest.
3. **DRIPCYCLEX Tactical Matte Bomber** — Water-repellent technical poly shell with silver industrial hardware.

## Architecture & Responsive Structure

- **Homepage (`/`)**:
  - Hero Campaign with draconic atmosphere and stats.
  - The Trinity Drop Showcase (custom 3-column responsive grid).
  - Interactive Silhouette Anatomy deep-dive tab switcher.
  - Brand Philosophy & Editorial manifesto.
  - Physical Concept Spaces locator teaser.
  - DRIPCYCLEX Syndicate VIP Drop subscription.
- **Shop (`/shop`)**:
  - Curated 3-column product showcase with responsive filters (Silhouettes, Colorways, Sizes, Price thresholds).
- **Product Details (`/product/$slug`)**:
  - Gallery, interactive size guide modal, colorway selectors, and "Complete The Trinity Capsule" featuring the other 2 pieces.
- **Brand Story (`/about`)**:
  - The origin, four core pillars, and domestic fabric milling standards.
- **Concept Spaces (`/stores`)**:
  - Filterable directory of 8 flagship spaces across Mumbai, Bengaluru, Delhi, and other metro hubs.
- **Client Concierge (`/contact`)**:
  - Client service inquiries, direct lines, and FAQ directives.

## Tech Stack

- **Framework**: TanStack Start + React 19 + Vite
- **Styling**: Tailwind CSS v4 + Tailwind Animate
- **Components**: Radix UI + Lucide Icons + Sonner Toasts
- **State**: TanStack Router + React Query + LocalStorage Shop Store
